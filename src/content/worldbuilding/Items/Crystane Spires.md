## Crystane Spires

Crystane Spires are crystalline constructs used throughout the county of [[County/Firbrook|Firbrook]] to draw and transmit ambient magical energy. These spires, mounted on rooftops, towers, or embedded into stonework, form part of the larger Crystal Conduit—an arcane network powered by ancient magical veins buried deep beneath the land.

The Spires are shaped from harmonized crystal and installed using a specially prepared Binding Resin. When activated through spellcraft, the resin attunes the crystal to the flow of leyline energy and anchors it to nearby Anchor Nodes—ritual stones that act as stabilizers and collection points for the energy rising from the depths.

These veins, still brimming with volatile power, are remnants of the Great Battle of [[Settlement/Tundyrj|Tun'dyrj]], a cataclysm whose scars and secrets continue to shape Firbrook to this day.

Crystane Spires are used to warm homes, illuminate halls, relay sigils across distances, and empower local enchantments. Though largely reliable, Spires may flicker or dim during geomantic disturbances, or when improperly maintained.

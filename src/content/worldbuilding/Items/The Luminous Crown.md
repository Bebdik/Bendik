## Description 

A diadem said to be forged from the light of a dying star, it grants the wearer the ability to see through darkness, both literal and metaphorical, revealing hidden truths and guiding them with divine insight. 

## Attributes

The Luminous Crown forms around the wearers head resembling two wings. It will always fit its wearer no matter what shape or size their head is. In the center of the piece the crown holds a bright light, believed to be salvaged from the dying star that gave life to it. The crown allows the wearer to see through the dark.

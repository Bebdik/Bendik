## Description

The Bouge were once very similar to the bugs of the real world in both their size and their abundance. Everything changed when their abundance became a pest, so much so, that a witch or wizard cursed them to be bigger in size in an effort to make them easier to kill (this made themlarger targets). Many bouge were killed in this genocide. However, a side effect of this growth curse was that it also affected their brains in such a way that they became a more intelligent species. The bouge are capable of culture and education amongst other things. As a result of this, bouge became very pleasant creatures to be around. Today, a lot of them have integrated themselves in general society; while certain groups prefer the more primal lifestyle in their place of origin (the swamps of [[Mirelith]], in which they are still considered abundant), others have taken root in large cities, often working as herbalists, alchemists, and doctors, some bouge have become politicians. Their developed language includes them addressing each other (and also other creatures to some extent) as "bug", "bugmeister", "buggart", or some variation of those.

## Physiology

They take the form of most real bugs, primarily cockroaches, ladybugs, grasshoppers, ants, flies, bees, mosquitoes, and beetles. 
Bouge share the capabilities of their real-life counterparts, and can therefore possess abilities such as flight, bioluminescence, and so on.

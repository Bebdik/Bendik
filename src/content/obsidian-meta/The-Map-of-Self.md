##  Use these questions to locate your current position within the symbolic system:

<br>

> ### I. Direction of Energy (Reflective ↔ Expressive)
> 
> 1. Are you trying to understand yourself, or interact with the world?
>    - Understanding → **Reflective**  
>    - Acting or engaging → **Expressive**  
> 
> 2. Would this moment still feel complete if no one saw or heard it?
>    - Yes → **Reflective**  
>    - No → **Expressive**  
> 
> 3. Does your energy feel like a dialogue with yourself, or a gesture toward something else?
>    - Dialogue → **Reflective**  
>    - Gesture → **Expressive**  
> 
> ---
> 
> ### II. Cognitive Style (Structured ↔ Intuitive)
> 
> 1. Are you trying to make sense of something, or are you following a feeling or image?
>    - Making sense → **Structured**  
>    - Following feeling → **Intuitive**  
> 
> 2. Are you organizing thoughts, or letting them move freely?
>    - Organizing → **Structured**  
>    - Drifting → **Intuitive**  
> 
> 3. Would a framework or a poem help you more right now?
>    - Framework → **Structured**  
>    - Poem → **Intuitive**
> 
> ---

## Deepen your understanding of your current state of mind:

<br>

> #### Reflective + Structured → Architect Self
> - What internal system or vision am I quietly refining?
> - What kind of structure brings me comfort right now?
> - Am I honoring the process even if it isn't visible?
> 
> #### Reflective + Intuitive → Witness Self
> - What inner image or emotion am I sitting with?
> - Can I allow mystery to exist without resolving it?
> - What does this moment *feel like*, symbolically?
> 
> #### Expressive + Structured → Cartographer Self
> - What idea am I trying to shape into clarity?
> - What form does this expression want to take?
> - How can I translate vision into something shareable?
> 
> #### Expressive + Intuitive → Liminal Fool Self
> - What impulse or emotion wants to move through me?
> - Can I express without needing to explain?
> - What would happen if I turned this confusion into a game?
